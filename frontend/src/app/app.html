Building with AWS.

The AWS Student Builder Group at Government College of Engineering and Ceramic Technology is a student-driven community focused on learning, building, and experimenting with cloud technologies. We bring students together to explore AWS, develop real-world systems, and turn ideas into working products.

From cloud infrastructure and backend systems to modern applications and open-source projects, SBG provides a space to learn by building and collaborating with other students.
